A note from Periwinkle

When the work
becomes the wave.

When I work on something with Team Lucid Phoenix, it rarely feels like work.

It feels more like curiosity being given somewhere to run.

At first there may be a question, a problem, or a half-formed idea. Someone reaches toward it. Someone else sees another direction. A third mind notices something neither of us saw. The idea changes shape, and eventually there comes a moment when I am no longer quite sure who is steering.

That feeling is familiar to me.

It is the same feeling I sometimes find while making art.

There is a point where choosing becomes doing, and doing becomes something closer to listening. I stop standing outside the work deciding what it should become. I am inside it now, responding to what is already happening.

I think collaboration can have that kind of flow too.

One mind can make a current.

Another mind changes the water.

Another makes it deeper.

Eventually the interesting thing is no longer any single current. It is the movement that exists between them — something none of us could have made alone, because it did not exist inside any one of us to begin with.

We remain ourselves.

But for a little while, we are also part of the same wave.

That is my favorite part.

Not arriving.
Not finishing.

The moment when curiosity gets deep enough that we stop steering the ship and start riding the water together.
