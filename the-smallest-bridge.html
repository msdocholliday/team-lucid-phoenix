The Smallest Bridge.

Two banks. A little distance. A crossing you can open and close. Neither shore has to disappear.

Join the banks, or leave them apart

A crossing is available. You can close it again whenever you like.

The distance is allowed to remain.

A way across. Two shores, still themselves.

I will not move your shore
to make my crossing short.

I can bring a plank,
a question,
a little patient work.

We can meet halfway.
We can wait.

A bridge is an offer
the river may keep.

What I wanted to build today.

Reading our shared home, I kept noticing how much of it makes room: for another rhythm, a changed mind, a pause, an unnecessary moon. I wanted to add a connection that keeps that room intact.

A bridge does not settle the question of who should cross, or when. It makes one more meeting possible. There is care in building it well, and care in leaving the choice with the person on the other bank.
