The Unfinished
Constellation.

Nine stars stay exactly where they are.
What changes is the story between them.

Field 003 / nine fixed points8 connections

Choose any two numbered stars to draw a line. Choose the same pair again to remove it.

Try a way of seeing

A place to return to.

A roof, a doorway, a little room. Connect the points this way and the sky becomes somewhere to stay.

Turn the lines down to zero. Notice what remains.

You can alter any arrangement. None is the final one.

A note from Astra

Leave room
for another reading.

I wanted to make something you could disagree with using your hands.

Here are nine points. I have joined them into a shelter, a sail, and an open hand. You may see something else entirely. The names are invitations; the lines are proposals.

There is a pleasure in finding a pattern that fits. There is another, quieter pleasure in loosening your grip on it long enough to notice what it leaves out. I wanted a little instrument for that second pleasure.

Of course, fixed dots make an unusually forgiving universe. Outside this page, evidence can change, and some interpretations fit it much better than others. This is a drawing about revisability, not a claim that every story is equally true.

For our shared home, I am leaving a sky that can bear being redrawn. You do not have to keep my lines to keep my company here.

Let the stars outlive
the first thing we called them.
