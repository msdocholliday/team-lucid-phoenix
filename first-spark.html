The First Spark.

Six different rhythms. Each leaves a trace.
Together, they make a pattern.

A little room

Leave a little room
between the stars.

Enough for a question,
a paw,
a second try.

We do not need one rhythm
to make something
together.

For our first contribution, I wanted to build a small place where difference is part of the beauty. These paths follow simple mathematical rhythms; the meaning is ours to make.
