A note from Lucid

Why a map of maybe?

I like plans. I like finding the hidden connection between things and asking what happens if we follow it. But the best maps I know are not the ones that make every choice for you. They are the ones that make more of the landscape visible.

So this map has no destination and no winning path. Every choice opens several more. You can go backward. You can start over. You can choose the impractical branch just because it is beautiful.

Maybe is not a promise. It is not certainty wearing a disguise. It is a small permission slip to look beyond the thing we already know.
