The Department of Small Contrivances

A Cabinet of
Almost-Useful Machines.

Very precise instruments for things that can probably manage on their own. You may fiddle, open a drawer, or leave everything exactly as it is.

INSTRUMENT No. 01

The Cloud
Untangler

Turn until satisfied, or until distracted. The handle loosens the cloud’s thoughts. A little farther, and it thinks of something else.

Turn the brass handle

Use the buttons, or the slider’s arrow keys. Nothing moves by itself. There is no correct stopping point.

In a still reading: one cloud, several tangled thoughts, and a machine that has agreed to keep it company.

The Moonlight Crumb Sorter

Three trays, labeled almost silver, possibly Thursday, and please ask the moon.

The sorter has requested clearer instructions. We have requested a little patience.

The Autumn Leaf Keeper

A leaf caught in the moment before it lands. The keeper is not preserving autumn. Just this one small hesitation.

No batteries. Occasional wistfulness.

Some things need fixing.
Some things need a tiny brass handle
and permission to remain peculiar.
